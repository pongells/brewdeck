# R4 Ready hold: after a heat-up the machine's readiness flips READY / HEATING every 10 s (seen on a cold start); the
# screen shows Ready only after 30 s of steady READY (the UI's clock at 10x: fast 10).
# checks: R4
wait 500
wait_for screen.ready 10000
fast 10
machine readiness 2
wait 15000
expect_text value.state Heating 100
repeat 3
machine readiness 3
wait 10000
expect_no_text value.state Ready 100
machine readiness 2
wait 10000
end
machine readiness 3
wait 20000
expect_no_text value.state Ready 100
expect_text value.state Ready 15000
fast 1
machine readiness -1
quit
